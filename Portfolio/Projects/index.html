Technological Acceptability of a Predictive Chemical Toxicity Software for Science-Related Programs in a Catholic School in Davao City

A descriptive-comparative research study evaluating the technological acceptance of ProTox 3.0—an online chemical toxicity prediction software—among 121 undergraduate students across laboratory-based science programs in Davao City. Results demonstrated high overall user acceptability, emphasizing the effectiveness of digitalizing lab safety information to increase student awareness regardless of academic level.
